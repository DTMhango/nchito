# Ashby

Hosts: `jobs.ashbyhq.com/<company>/<uuid>`; the form is on the "Application" tab
(`.../<uuid>/application`). Some employers embed it on their own careers domain.

- One page, no account. Switching to the "Application" tab is fine.
- Resume upload may auto-fill fields ("Autofill from resume"). Do not use autofill; if fields
  were filled anyway, overwrite them from the packet and clear any the packet does not have.
- Many questions are custom: yes/no toggles, multi-select chips, and free text. For chips,
  select exactly the options `find_answer` returns; never extra ones.
- Location may be a type-ahead; pick the suggestion that matches the packet value exactly.
- Ashby forms often have a "Survey" (EEO) section after the main questions: `find_answer`,
  normally `do_not_answer`.
- Confirmation: "Thanks for applying" / "Your application has been submitted" replaces the
  form.
